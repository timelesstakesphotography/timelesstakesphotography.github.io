Approach

For most of the day, you’ll barely notice we’re there. We let things unfold and quietly catch them as they happen.

When you need us, for portraits or gathering the whole family, we step in and make it easy, so no one feels stiff in front of the camera.

Afterwards, every image is edited by hand, with natural skin tones and a soft, timeless finish. Your gallery reads like the story of your day, from the first nervous minutes to the last dance. It’s something you’ll sit down and go through together, again and again.

About

About Timeless Takes.

Timeless Takes is a London wedding photography studio. It began with a single wedding and a couple who trusted us with their day, and it has grown through recommendation ever since.

We photograph weddings across faiths and cultures, from temple halls to register offices to windswept clifftops. Whatever the setting, the approach is the same: real moments, framed with care, in natural colour that won’t date. Pictures that will feel as right in thirty years as they do today.
